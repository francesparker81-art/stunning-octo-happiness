## Reimagining Canvas Around Time, Not Courses
4
 
5
Canvas Flow is an AI-assisted redesign of the Canvas Learning Management System focused on helping students manage their workload through a weekly planning experience rather than navigating individual courses.
6
 
7
Instead of organizing information by class, Canvas Flow organizes information around what students actually care about:
8
 
9
- What is due next?
10
- What should I work on today?
11
- How much time will my week require?
12
- What projects need team collaboration?
13
- What can wait?
14
 
15
## Problem Statement
16
 
17
Traditional learning management systems organize content by course. While effective for instructors, students often have to navigate multiple classes to understand their overall workload.
18
 
19
This creates several challenges:
20
 
21
- Assignments are scattered across courses
22
- Students struggle to prioritize tasks
23
- Workload is difficult to visualize
24
- Calendar integration is limited
25
- Collaboration tools are disconnected
26
- Important deadlines can be overlooked
27
 
28
